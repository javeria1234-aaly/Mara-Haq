# Haq-e-Wirasat: Women's Inheritance Rights & Legal Procedure Portal (Pakistan)
An open-source legal-tech awareness platform designed to empower women in Pakistan with actionable, step-by-step procedural guidance to claim, trace, protect, and execute their statutory and Islamic rights of inheritance (Wirasat).
**Project Overview**
Many women in Pakistan face systemic and procedural barriers when claiming their rightful share in ancestral or familial property—ranging from hidden assets and omitted names in family trees to fraudulent relinquishment deeds (Tanazul-nama). 

This platform bridges the gap between complex statutory procedures and everyday citizens by providing a clear, bilingual-friendly roadmap covering everything from initial asset discovery to final mutation (Intiqal) and civil court litigation.
## Core Legal Modules Covered
Tracing Unknown Deceased Assets & Succession including Movable Assets like Dormant Funds & Equities, Vehicle, Bank Debit Freezes, Excise Caveats and Immovable Property. 
 Fraud & (Tanazul) Risk Detector for Protecting of uneducated Female Heirs For example depriving women through illegal means like sign on will deed or gift deed by wrongful intent. For that purpose Bilingual Red-Flag Document Checklist is available.
 Administrative Remedies & Valuation Tools for record error. i.e; checking in alpha tree for FRC.
  Step-by-Step Civil Trial & Statutory Enforcement Process.
 ## Legal Disclaimer 
 The information provided on this website and repository is strictly for public legal awareness and educational purposes. It does not constitute formal legal representation or an attorney-client relationship. Users facing active property disputes or fraudulent deprivation of inheritance are advised to consult a licensed advocate or approach their respective District Legal Empowerment Committee (DLEC) / Ombudsperson for Protection Against Harassment of Women in Property Rights.
